# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis | SQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Pizza Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing one year of pizza sales data (January–December 2015) to evaluate business performance, identify customer ordering patterns, and uncover actionable business insights.

Using SQL for data analysis and Power BI for interactive visualization, this project explores key performance indicators (KPIs), revenue trends, product performance, and customer purchasing behavior.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales performance and revenue.
- Analyze daily, monthly, and seasonal ordering trends.
- Identify top-performing and low-performing pizza products.
- Understand customer preferences across pizza categories and sizes.
- Monitor key business performance indicators.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve sales.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
- Calculated total revenue and average order value.
- Evaluated total orders and pizza quantities sold.
- Analyzed overall business performance.

### 2. Time-Based Analysis
- Examined daily order trends.
- Identified the busiest days of the week.
- Analyzed monthly sales patterns.
- Identified peak and low-demand months.

### 3. Product Performance Analysis
- Identified the top 5 pizzas by revenue.
- Identified the top 5 pizzas by quantity sold.
- Identified the top 5 pizzas by total orders.
- Analyzed the bottom 5 pizzas across these metrics.

### 4. Category & Size Analysis
- Compared sales contributions across pizza categories.
- Evaluated customer preferences by pizza size.
- Identified the highest-performing categories and sizes.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue Performance:** The business generated 817.86K in total revenue.
2. **Daily Trends:** Friday recorded the highest order volume at approximately 3.5K.
3. **Monthly Trends:** July recorded the highest monthly orders with 1,935 orders.
4. **Category Performance:** Classic pizzas contributed the highest sales share at 26.91%.
5. **Size Preference:** Large pizzas accounted for 45.89% of sales.
6. **Top Revenue Products:** Thai Chicken and Barbecue Chicken generated approximately 43K each.
7. **Top Quantity Product:** Classic Deluxe recorded the highest quantity sold at approximately 2.5K.
8. **Low Performance:** Brie Carre recorded the lowest revenue at approximately 12K.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

- Introduce special promotional campaigns on Fridays to maximize sales.
- Focus on Large and Medium pizza sizes based on customer preferences.
- Promote high-revenue products such as Thai Chicken and Barbecue Chicken.
- Introduce combo offers featuring Classic Deluxe pizzas.
- Investigate customer preferences to improve low-performing products.
- Develop targeted promotions during low-demand months.
